# 3-minute demo video script

**Format:** screen recording of the PWA + voiceover. Subtitle in English.

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] The hook — show the gap
*Camera: stock footage of a rural health post, then cut to a paper IMCI booklet.*

> "A community health worker in rural Tanzania has minutes to decide whether this
> child needs urgent referral. She has a paper booklet, no specialist on call, and
> no internet. Today, we built her a partner."

### [0:20 – 0:35] Reveal — show the app cold
*Cut to a tablet running Sahayak. Airplane mode toggle ON. Loud beep.*

> "This is Sahayak. It runs entirely on her tablet, in airplane mode, on Gemma 4."

### [0:35 – 1:30] The case — Amina, 10 months, Swahili
*Click **Load sample**.*

> "She enters a real case: a 10-month-old, refusing to feed, fast breathing, low
> oxygen. She types in Swahili — the language she speaks with the caregiver."

*Click **Run triage**. Cursor hovers over the trace pane while it streams.*

> "Watch what Gemma 4 does. It doesn't guess. First it calls a local function that
> checks WHO IMCI danger signs. Then it classifies the case…"

*RED badge appears.*

> "…RED. Refer immediately. Then it generates a referral note — in Swahili — that
> she can hand to the receiving facility."

### [1:30 – 2:10] Why this is different
*Split-screen: trace pane on left, model reply on right.*

> "Two things make this safe enough to deploy. One: the clinical rules — danger
> signs, dosing — are local deterministic functions. Gemma 4 cannot invent them.
> Two: every decision is auditable. The trace pane shows every tool call. A
> supervising clinician can review exactly why a case was classified RED."

### [2:10 – 2:40] The scale shot
*Cut to a globe / map showing low-income regions. Numbers fade in:*

> "There are over **3.4 million** community health workers worldwide. Most have
> never had an AI tool that speaks their language, works offline, and follows their
> protocols. Gemma 4 makes that possible — open, on-device, today."

### [2:40 – 3:00] The close
*Back to the tablet. CHW closes it, walks out of frame.*

> "Sahayak. Built on Gemma 4. So that the right tool is always the one that's
> already in her hand."
